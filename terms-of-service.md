## 1. What this is

Lights Out is an iPhone app that helps you go to bed earlier. It works out a
"lights out" time backwards from when you want to wake up, it can block apps you
choose in the evening, it sets your alarm, and it scores the night afterwards
from what Apple Health recorded.

By using it you agree to these terms. If you do not, do not use the app.

## 2. It is not a medical device

**This is the important one.**

Lights Out is a habit app. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose
anything, and it does not treat anything.

- The sleep scores, stage breakdowns and recovery figures are **estimates**,
  computed from what Apple Health recorded, which is itself estimated by your
  watch or phone.
- They are there to make last night comparable to your other nights. They are
  not a measurement of your health.
- **If you are worried about your sleep, talk to a doctor.** Do not use a number
  in this app instead of that, and do not change anything a doctor told you
  because of one.

## 3. The alarm

Lights Out sets a real system alarm through Apple's AlarmKit, which is why it can
ring through silent mode and through a Focus, the way the Clock app does.

It is still software running on a phone. **Do not rely on it alone for something
you cannot afford to miss.** It will not ring if the phone is off, out of
battery, or if you revoked the alarm permission. We do not guarantee that it
rings, and we are not responsible for what happens if it does not.

## 4. Blocking apps

If you switch on the evening block, Lights Out uses Apple's Screen Time to block
the apps you chose, at the times you set. It only ever blocks what you picked.

You can switch it off, change the selection, or remove Lights Out's Screen Time
permission in Settings at any time. Deleting your account lifts every block the
app placed.
